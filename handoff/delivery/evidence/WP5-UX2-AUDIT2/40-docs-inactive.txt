docs/04_UX_AND_SETTINGS.md:54:- Today: always shown. It opens the period that contains today's date in the timesheet's saved reporting zone (the server's date, never the device zone), shows today's column and moves an open Day editor to today. It is inactive (`aria-disabled`, still focusable, dimmed with colours) when the sheet already shows today and the editor, if open, is on today; a press on the inactive button re-reads the server's date and moves to today's period only when the date has changed (for example after midnight in the reporting zone), otherwise nothing moves. The periods are also re-read when the window regains focus or the tab becomes visible.
