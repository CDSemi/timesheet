docs/12_RELEASE_NOTES.md:114:- **A correction's send can wait behind an older send.** A send of one revision of a period is refused (409, "A delivery of this period is already in progress") while a send job of another revision of the same period still waits, so the older copy cannot arrive after the correction's. While the mail server is down, the older job keeps retrying on the ladder 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes (about 81 minutes in all) and cannot be cancelled, so the correction's send can wait that long ([05 Submission](05_SUBMISSION_AND_NOTIFICATIONS.md) "Corrections and resends"). After a restore the same rule holds the release of a held send of an older revision (`superseded_by_later_send`).
