docs/10_DECISIONS_AND_SOURCES.md:315:- The owner's reason rule (F-4, plan B-A2). A copy counts as sent once any attempt of any revision of the period was `accepted` or `uncertain`; a send whose attempts all failed permanently does not count. When a copy counts, another send, and the "resend" decision on an uncertain attempt, need a reason of 2-500 characters (trimmed, NFC, no control characters except line feeds), else 422 `reason_required`. The reason is stored on the send and in its audit event and is never added to the email. The check runs inside the send transaction. The reason not being in the email, a failed first send needing no reason, and the uncertain resend needing one are the planner's reversible defaults (plan Addendum 1, E revised).
