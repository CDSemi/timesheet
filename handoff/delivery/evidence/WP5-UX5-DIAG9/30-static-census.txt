The one test that crosses the midnight on purpose is today.spec.ts:626 ("a page left open across a reporting-zone midnight"). It starts within 90 s of the
midnight (the gate starts it at 23:58:30 to 23:59:20), waits for it with a poll and asserts both tabs. In REGATE9 its desktop run took 1.1 m (it crossed) and
its mobile run 2.7 s (it started after the midnight and took the real-clock branch). It must keep crossing, so the fix exempts it by annotation.
