E. Breaking changes (nodemailer CHANGELOG.md of 10.2.1, entries 10.0.15 .. 10.2.1; zod: patch releases 4.6.6 and 4.6.7 of 4.6.5)
  All are minor or patch releases of the 10.x line; there is no major bump. Entries that touch what the app uses:
  - 10.1.0 "harden message streams, SMTP connections and the connection pool" plus SMTP fixes: end the session on any 421,
    refuse a second connect() call, drop the connection when DATA follows a refused sender, count the greeting wait against
    connectionTimeout. The app opens one SMTPConnection per message and calls connect() once (smtpAdapter.ts:79, :132), and
    the SMTP adapter tests and the smoke against the in-repo smtp-server sink pass, so no adjustment is needed.
  - 10.1.4 addressparser corrections (the three advisories plus group-nesting bound and others): parser only, unreachable.
  - 10.1.2 "bound the work a message can make the mailer do": not triggered by the app's messages (the probe is byte-identical).
  - 10.2.0 adds optional features (info.serverMessageId, err.permanent, pendingCount); the app ignores them.
  No deprecation notice for the APIs the app uses (MailComposer, SMTPConnection). zod: no changelog entry was read for 4.6.6
  and 4.6.7 beyond the advisory (GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5 first patched 4.6.6); the verified behaviour is the test result above.
