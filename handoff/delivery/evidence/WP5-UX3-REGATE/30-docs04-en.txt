| App shell | Desktop top bar: the C&D logo, with the two-line wordmark ("SEMICONDUCTOR" over "SERVICES, INC.") beside it from 1024px (hidden from 1024 to 1279px only while the "Shared with me" switcher is in the bar; logo only below 1024px and on phones), then Timesheet, Overtime and History, and at the right end the identity block: the display name in bold over the email in smaller muted text (both right-aligned and cut with an ellipsis at 14rem), an initials avatar and a chevron. The identity block always shows the signed-in person, also in a shared view, and opens the account menu, a disclosure panel (not an ARIA menu, so its links, Theme choice and button keep their own semantics): a header with the avatar, the full name, the email and, for administrators, "Administrator"; Profile, Settings, Administration (administrators only), the Light/Dark "Theme" control and Sign out. Opened by keyboard it focuses the first entry; Escape closes it and returns focus to the identity button; a press outside, a page change or Tab past Sign out also closes it. After an entry is chosen, focus moves to the new page's main heading, or back to the identity button (on phones, More) when the page does not change; the heading shows the focus ring only to keyboard users. The brand is not a link; its text name "C&D Semiconductor Services, Inc." is hidden text read once by screen readers. The space from the brand to the menu is 24px, and 32px wherever the wordmark shows. Phone: compact top bar with the logo and, on the person's own screens, the check in/out controls (see "Period bar, check in/out and batch mode"), no avatar, and a bottom tab bar with Timesheet, Overtime, History and More; More holds the same account content (header, Profile, Settings, Import, Administration for administrators only, the "Theme" control and Sign out). Import (the person's own workbook and opening balance) is reached from a link in Settings on the desktop and from More on a phone; its address keeps working. The Review belongs under Timesheet and has no entry of its own |
