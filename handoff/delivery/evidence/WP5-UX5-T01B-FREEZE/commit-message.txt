Freeze WP5-UX5-T01B: sign off and send are separate acts

- feat(signoff): sign-off, corrections and late reviews create the signed
  revision and its PDF only. Nothing is sent by signing.
- feat(sends): each send is its own immutable record.
  - It carries To, Cc, subject, body and the Time and OT row switches.
  - It is idempotent by request key.
  - The send job builds the email from that record.
- feat(sends): once a copy of a period has been accepted or is uncertain,
  another send needs a confirmation and a typed reason. The reason is
  2-500 characters, audited and never put in the email. (Owner rule F-4.)
- feat(pdf): up to four stored PDF variants per revision (Time and OT rows
  on or off), with a "Time rows on the PDF" default in Settings.
  (Owner decision F-3 b.)
- feat(automation): at the deadline, with auto-submit on, a signed but
  unsent period is sent; with auto-submit off, it is marked overdue.
  Reminders stop at the first send. (Owner decision F-2 a.)
- feat(db): migration 0015 adds the PDF variants, the send records with a
  backfill for existing sends, and the new Settings column.
- feat(ui): the Review page signs off, then sends with the defaults, until
  the Send card arrives; History resends with a reason.
- test: a new sends suite, a v14 upgrade test, and adapted integration,
  fixture and e2e tests; five mutants caught.
- docs: docs/01 (FR-11), 03, 05, 06, 10 and DEVELOPMENT (+ vi).
- docs(handoff): the T01A-FREEZE results, the T01B record and evidence,
  the board and the checkpoint (+ vi).
