69:- Switching and unselecting: a press on another column's day button, or anywhere on that column, switches the drawer to that day without closing it first; a press (or Enter) on the open day's own button closes it, focus stays on that button and a polite status says "Day editor closed". When the Day fields form or the reason holds edits that are not saved, every action that would change the open day, close the drawer or move the sheet to another period first asks "Discard your changes?" (Keep editing and Discard) through the confirmation dialog: a switch, an unselect, "Open a day", "Today", "Previous period", "Next period", "Current period", the Close button and Escape (a nested dialog keeps Escape first). Keep editing leaves the drawer, its edits and the period shown exactly as they were; Discard goes ahead (a period step leaves the drawer open and starts it again from the saved day). With nothing unsaved, nothing asks. A press outside closes the editor the same way (round 5, U5-24, owner answer F-7 a): on a desktop (768px and wider) a `pointerdown` on the page closes the drawer through Close's path, with the same question, unless it lands inside a day column (`[data-day]`), the drawer, any dialog or an interactive control (button, link, input, select, textarea, label, switch, option), so a control keeps its own action ("Today" still moves the open editor, another day column switches the day); a press that is not the primary button, or lands on a scrollbar (the window's, or the sideways scrollbar of a week card), is not a press on the page. A touch press acts when the finger lifts: a swipe that starts outside a day column scrolls the page, the browser cancels that touch, and nothing closes. On a phone a tap on the bottom sheet's backdrop (the dialog element itself, outside its box) closes it. The press is consumed, so focus is not moved to the page afterwards: Keep editing returns focus to the field that had it and a close returns it to the day's button. Two routes do not ask, by design: a link that leaves the page (the app has no navigation guard on any screen), and the reload after a label or batch commit that changed the open day (the person changed that day on purpose, so the form follows its new saved version and the fields typed for that day are dropped). Saved fields and a reason already used by a save are not unsaved edits. A Check in or Check out of the page, which the non-modal drawer leaves usable, makes the open editor read its day again, and so does the tab coming back into view: the sessions and figures follow and what was typed stays, with no question (a change made elsewhere to the day's fields is not taken in this way: the save is refused and "Reload day" reads it). The same holds for the answer of a session save, "Confirm breaks" or a delete: a change made elsewhere to the day's fields is never carried under a newer version; the day fields start again from the answer when nothing is typed in them and otherwise keep what was typed, so their save is refused and "Reload day" reads the change. A calendar default that changed elsewhere (a holiday import) is refused at the save in the same way and answered by the notice described in the "Changed calendar default" bullet below. The session dialog is modal, so no switch can happen while it is open. Focus moves to the drawer heading on open; the drawer follows the sheet in the DOM (Tab from the sheet reaches it, Shift+Tab leaves it); Escape closes the drawer wherever focus is (a nested dialog takes Escape first), as does Close, and focus returns to the day's date button. While the drawer is open, the day it edits is marked on the sheet (see "Timesheet sheet").
72:- Changed calendar default (owner decision 2026-11-02, R-UX5I2-3; rule: [02 Time and OT](02_TIME_AND_OT_RULES.md) R-03, contract: [03 Architecture](03_ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATA.md) "API and hosting boundary"): the day fields form remembers the calendar default it showed when it opened (never the live value of the day behind it) and sends it with every save. When the save is refused because the default of the date changed since (for example an administrator's holiday import while the editor was open), nothing is saved and a notice appears in "Category and leave", under its heading and above the fields, in the same slot in the desktop drawer, the phone sheet and a shared edit view: "Notice. The calendar default for this day changed from {old or none} to {new} since it was loaded. Nothing was saved. Use the new default, or save your category over it." The notice is an alert, takes focus and is scrolled into view, so the phone's foot does not hide it. It has two buttons, in this Tab order, both 44px targets below 768px and with a coarse pointer and with the shared focus ring: "Use {new}" (secondary) and "Save {category} anyway" (primary, where {category} is the category in the form now). The state is carried by the words, not by colour. "Use {new}" sends nothing: the Category takes the new default, every other typed field stays, the form's saved baseline is rebased so that taking the default alone is not an unsaved edit (a typed note still is, and still triggers "Discard your changes?"), a polite status says "Category set to {new}, the calendar default now. Nothing needs saving." (or "... Save day fields to keep your other changes."), focus goes to the Category select, and the page and the editor read the day once so the sheet, chips and figures show the new default. "Save {category} anyway" repeats the save with the typed fields and the new default named, so the person's category is recorded over it; it is disabled while the reason of an old or finalized period is missing, and on success focus goes to the Category select. A further change of the default shows a new notice; a changed entry version shows the stale notice and "Reload day" instead, which wins over this notice. Choosing the new default by hand and pressing Save is accepted without a notice, and so is a save of a different category after "Use". The hint "Calendar default. Saving records it as your choice." stays. Nothing is refused for an explicit category (a saved choice never follows the calendar), for a date whose default did not change, or when the category saved equals the new default.
81:- Category in the cell: on an editable sheet the Category cell is a picker (Worked, Off, Vacation, Sick, Holiday, Shutdown and "Work from home" = Worked with WFH) operated by mouse or keyboard. The open picker has a 1px accent line, the active option (keyboard or pointer) has a light fill and an accent rail on its leading edge without a frame, and the current value is shown in accent text with a check. A pick is first sent as a one-entry batch preview and commits immediately only when the preview needs no reason and shows no conflict with recorded work; otherwise the existing review dialog ("Review category change for {date}") asks for the confirmation or reason. The picker is absent in a view-only share, in batch mode and for an imported period. A date whose calendar default changed since the sheet was loaded is refused like a changed day: the review dialog marks it "calendar default changed" and commits nothing, and closing it offers "Reload period" so the cell shows the new default; picking again on the reloaded sheet saves the person's category over it, now seen.
