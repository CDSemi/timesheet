## Risks and optional improvements (not proven defects)

- **R-UX5B2-1, DIAG4's product note is wider than "right after sign-off".** The job queue orders by `next_run_at`, `created_at` (whole seconds), then a random id (`jobStore.ts:243`), so any send whose PDF variant is not stored yet is created in the same second as that PDF job and is claimed first half of the time: 15 of 30 runs right after the sign-off, and 15 of 30 runs of a later send with a row switch off (`40-sends-probe-*`, R1, R2). The send then waits the one-minute retry and spends one of its five attempts; nothing is lost or duplicated, the PDF is the right variant, and the job stays `queued` with `pdf_not_ready` until its retry (the administrator status shows that code meanwhile). Acceptable for the pilot (the owner sends from a page, not in the sign-off second, but turning a row switch off is a normal choice). Backlog: claim `render_pdf` before `send_email` at equal instants, or do not count `pdf_not_ready` as an attempt.
- **R-UX5B2-2, the Resend button does not see an older revision's waiting send.** `resendState` (`deliveryModel.ts:244-253`) reads only the current revision's jobs (`finalization.ts:260-262` returns the jobs of the current revision), so in the cross-revision case the button is enabled and the click returns 409 with "A delivery of this period is already in progress." (`deliveryModel.ts:376`). The string at `:251`, "A delivery of this revision is in progress.", is shown only when the revision's own attempt or job runs, so it is accurate where it appears, not misleading; but the comment "Mirrors the server's refusals" no longer holds for this case. Optional: a period-wide waiting flag from the server.
- **R-UX5B2-3, how long option A can block.** A correction's send waits as long as the older send job is queued or leased: seconds normally, up to the retry ladder (1, 5, 15, 60 minutes, about 81 minutes) when the mail server is down or the older PDF cannot be rendered, with no way to cancel the older send. docs/05:72 states the block but not its length; optional sentence in docs/12 known limits.
