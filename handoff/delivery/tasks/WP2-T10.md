## Required content (DayEditor and supporting components)

- **Manual sessions.** Each session has:
  - an explicit input zone;
  - a DST fold or offset choice when a local time is ambiguous;
  - a choice when a local time does not exist (the gap);
  - an explicit end date for overnight work.
  The client sends exactly what the server contract expects and computes no UTC instants
  for business rules beyond what the contract requires. Device-zone changes must not
  regroup anything.
- **Break suggestions** shifted by arrival (R-01/R-02):
  - confirm, edit and none actions;
  - unknown stays pending;
  - follow the E-1 contract from T01.
- **Clock in and Clock out**: break confirmation and `expected_version`.
- **Day fields**:
  - category;
  - partial leave minutes with `leave_kind` (vacation, sick or ot);
  - WFH.
  Show the E-2 warning when `ot_leave.mismatch` is true for the day. The warning only
  informs; the editor never spends or reserves anything.
- **Server figures**: expected finish, and raw, eligible and credited minutes.
- **Reason prompt** for old periods.
- **Stale-version conflict**: reload and say so.
- **T09B follow-up**:
  - Future days currently display as "missing record". Show days after the server's
    current local date as upcoming instead.
  - This is display mapping only, based on a server-provided date; never use the device
    clock for business decisions.
