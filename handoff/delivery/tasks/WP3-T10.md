#### Interpretations and decisions to confirm

1. The schema's name for the automatic origin is `deadline` (CHECK on `timesheet_revisions.origin`); the
   brief's "origin automatic" is stored as `deadline` and the ledger origin is `automatic`.
2. "Governing switch": a saved settings version applies to deadlines on or after its own effective instant,
   so the scan uses the newest version whose instant is not after the deadline (literal "current version"
   would leave a period between two switch changes neither submitted nor overdue). A deadline before every
   version is never touched; a user who never saved settings has the default (on).
3. The overdue "state and notice record" is an audit event, not a `reminder_occurrences` row or a job: T11
   owns the notice and its dedupe; T11 can read the records with `listOverdueRecords` or the audit query.
4. The route's zod body is defined inside `admin.ts` because `http/schemas.ts` is not owned here.
5. The activation may not be before the clock; this prevents recording a past instant that would sweep
   history into automation. A pilot that wants an earlier boundary clears and re-records at the right time.
