## Coordinator decision E-1 (binding)

At Clock out `breaks` is optional. If the list is omitted, the saved rows are kept, and
only an unconfirmed clock-out may omit it. If the list is present, it is the complete set
and replaces the saved rows, whether or not it is confirmed.

## Required changes

1. Clock out requires `expected_version`; a mismatch returns 409 `stale_version` and
   rolls back.
2. On open sessions, a break end must be ≤ now + 5 minutes. This applies to break
   create, update and Clock out. Use a named constant and a typed 422 error.
3. Legacy future break rows at Clock out get a defined, typed outcome, documented in your
   report.
4. Add regressions for:
   - fault injection after the DELETE, where rollback restores the original rows and IDs;
   - the zero-row UPDATE branch, which returns 409.
5. The client sends the version at Clock out.
6. The six existing F-01 regressions stay green.

Rows are never dropped silently (immutable history, R-01/R-02). A break ending inside the 5-minute allowance, but after the actual Clock out instant, is covered by the same code.
