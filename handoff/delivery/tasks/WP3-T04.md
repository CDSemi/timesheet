## Required changes (binding)

1. **Canonical JSON and hash** (`src/domain/canonical.ts`): stable key order, integer
   minutes, ISO dates, UTC instant strings, Unicode-stable (NFC), SHA-256 over the
   canonical bytes. No floating-point minutes.
2. **Snapshot builder** (`src/domain/snapshot.ts`, `src/server/services/reviewPayload.ts`):
   the payload named in the plan's T04 paragraph: the 14 days (category, leave minutes and
   kind, sessions and breaks, raw/eligible/credited minutes, completeness), policy and
   calendar version IDs, reporting zone, employee name, OT proposals per day, deficit
   proposals with the policy mode, unresolved inputs, unconsumed OT-leave reservations
   (E-3 flag), recipients with the rendered subject and body, the signature reference
   (attachment ID and hash, never bytes), template version and the show-OT flag. No
   credentials. All calculations come only from the existing engine; the snapshot
   computes no business minutes of its own.
3. **Review route**: `GET /api/timesheets/:payrollDate/review` returns the payload, its
   hash and the timesheet `expected_version`, and writes nothing. Another user's request
   gives 404; anonymous gives 401.
