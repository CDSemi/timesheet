the explicit resume. That conflicts with docs/07:30 ("Reconcile pending/uncertain jobs

**Binding decision (reversible by the owner):**
1. **Restore.** Every queued or leased outbound job of kind `send_email` or

   `cli.js outbound release --confirm`. The operator uses it after checking the source
   mailbox or the capture.

   - A held reminder may instead be dropped explicitly, also audited.

   They still wait for `resume`.
4. **Tests.** Write red-first tests:
   - a queued send in the backup is never sent after `resume` without a release;
   - a released job is sent exactly once;
   - a dropped reminder is never sent.

**Release and drop** (`src/server/ops/restore.ts`; CLI in `src/server/cli.ts`):

- Each release or drop is one IMMEDIATE transaction with a system audit event (`operations.held_job_release` or
  `operations.held_job_drop`; entity `job`, actor and owner NULL, kind/state/attempts only).

- `attempt_uncertain`: its latest attempt is uncertain, so the attempt takes the owner's decision instead;
- `delivery_attempt_open`: another attempt of the revision is open;
- `superseded_by_later_send`: a later send of the same revision is queued, leased or succeeded, so a send the owner
  repeated is never sent twice.

A released job then runs the normal send handler.
