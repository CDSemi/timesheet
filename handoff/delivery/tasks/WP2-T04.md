## Binding decisions and notes

- **E-6 (coordinator).** Provisional = credited minutes of complete days in unfinalized
  periods, shown per period.
- **E-13 (coordinator).** WP2 history = the user's own audit trail plus policy and
  calendar versions.
- **T03 note.** The otLeave service does not require actor = owner. Every route must
  pass the session user as both actor and owner. Admin is not blanket access to employee
  data.
- No route may post a credit or debit. A route-inventory test asserts this.

## Endpoints (from the plan)

- `GET /api/ot/summary`: posted, provisional, reserved, available, and the negative
  flag.
- `GET /api/ot/ledger`.
- `GET|POST /api/ot/leave`.
- `POST /api/ot/leave/:id/{consume,cancel,reverse}` with `expected_version`. "consume"
  is the record-use action from E-3.
- `GET /api/ot/evidence.csv?from&to`. Contents: raw intervals, breaks, policy versions,
  daily raw/eligible/credited minutes, ledger adjustments, and leave permission. Cells
  starting with `= + - @`, a tab or a CR are neutralized. The `Content-Disposition`
  filename is sanitized. Responses are `no-store` and owner-only.
- `GET /api/history`.

Covers FR-09, FR-14, AC-01, and AC-04 (history), plus "safe evidence export" and "no
arbitrary public credit endpoint".
