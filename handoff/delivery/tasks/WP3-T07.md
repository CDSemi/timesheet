#### What was built

- `layout.ts` (pure, no pdf-lib): page and margin constants (US Letter 612x792 pt), `SIGNATURE_BOX` 210x54 pt,
  `formatHoursMinutes` (h:mm, throws on negative or fractional minutes), `formatUsDate`, `cleanText` (control,
  format, bidi and separator characters become spaces), `wrapText` (greedy, long words broken by character),
  `fitText` (shrink from max to min size, then ellipsis on the last permitted line), `fitImage` (aspect ratio
  kept, never beyond the box), `sanitizeFileNamePart` and `timesheetPdfFileName` (ASCII `[A-Za-z0-9_]` only,
  Vietnamese reduced to base letters, 40 characters max, fallback `employee`, `_r<revision>` suffix).
- `fonts.ts`: loads DejaVu Sans and Bold from `node_modules` (path found with `createRequire`), cached.
- `timesheetPdf.ts`: `renderTimesheetPdf({snapshot, submissionId, revisionNo, origin, signedAt, signatureImage})`
  returns PDF bytes. It normalizes the snapshot (existing validator), requires exactly 14 consecutive days from the
  period start, and draws one US Letter portrait page: company header and the template title, submission and
  revision (with origin) lines, employee, payroll date, period, two blocks of Day / Date / Category / Time / OT
  rows for the 14 dates (weekday names from the dates, categories with the holiday name, session times in the
  saved reporting zone with `+1` for an overnight end and an open end shown as `08:00-`, OT cell from the
  snapshot's credited minutes or `pending` / `n/a`). "Overtime Total : H:MM h:mm" prints the snapshot's
  `totals.credited_minutes` (which includes both Sundays), plus a note when days with OT pending are not in it.
  When `show_ot_on_pdf` is off the OT rows and the total are not drawn. Employee Signature: the bounded image
  (PNG or JPEG by magic bytes, anything else refused), the employee name and the real sign date (the `signedAt`
  instant converted to a date in the reporting zone) only for a manual revision; Manager Signature and its Date
  stay blank. An automatic revision shows the "Employee review pending" banner, no signer name and no date,
  and an automatic input that carries `signedAt` is refused. The footer repeats submission and revision.
  Private day notes are never printed. No business minute is computed (only formatting of snapshot values).
- Determinism: `PDFDocument.create({updateMetadata: false})`, fixed Creator/Producer, creation and modification
  date taken from `period.due_at_utc`, fixed embedded font names, `useObjectStreams: false`. pdf-lib's resource
  names come from a seeded generator, so no override was needed (my first draft overrode the suffix function; a
  mutation proved it was unnecessary, so it was removed). The test spies `Math.random` and moves the clock.
