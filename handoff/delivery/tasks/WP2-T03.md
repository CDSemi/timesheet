## Binding decisions

- **Owner E-2.** OT leave is a leave request linked to a date. It is not a day category.
  A day-label or `leave_kind` change never reserves or spends (LG-10).
- **Owner E-3.** OT leave is consumed only through an explicit, idempotent employee
  "record use" service, on or after the leave date. Partial use is allowed. Any unused
  reservation stays reserved until it is used or cancelled.
- **Coordinator E-5.** Insufficient available balance at reservation → 409
  `insufficient_balance`; no reservation is created.
- **Coordinator E-7.** Permission evidence is a text reference (no files in WP2).

## Required content (from the plan)

- Record the manager permission (name/identity, date, evidence text, origin
  `self_recorded`). Reserve atomically under BEGIN IMMEDIATE, with an available check.
- Consume X ≤ reserved, with idempotent keys. Partial use keeps the remainder reserved,
  following E-3.
- Cancel releases unused minutes. Reversing used minutes posts a linked compensating
  +delta.
- Retries never append. Minutes convert 1:1 (480 for eight hours, not 510).
- Covers R-06, FR-09, AC-03, LG-03…LG-07 and LG-10 on production services and real
  SQLite.
