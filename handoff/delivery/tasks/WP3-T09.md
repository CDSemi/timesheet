## Required changes (binding)

1. **Dependencies.** Runtime `nodemailer` 10.0.14 (SMTP transport and MIME; ships its own
   types); dev `smtp-server` 3.19.17 and `@types/smtp-server` 3.5.13 for the loopback
   sink. Confirm none is deprecated and no deprecation warning appears; the lockfile adds
   only these and their dependencies.
2. **Message builder** (`src/server/mail/message.ts`): built only from the frozen snapshot
   (recipients, rendered subject and body) and the stored PDF bytes, whose hash is
   re-checked against `revision_files`; a stable Message-ID per attempt; no template
   rendering at send time.
3. **Adapters** (`outbound.ts`, `captureAdapter.ts`, `smtpAdapter.ts`): capture (the
   default) writes the exact .eml and the PDF under the private data directory; SMTP is
   used only when `OUTBOUND_MODE=smtp` and the owner-only production flag is set, and in
   tests only against the loopback sink; credentials come from env only and never appear
   in logs, errors, DB rows or responses (redacted).
4. **Send job** (`src/server/jobs/sendJob.ts`, registered in `runner.ts`): commit the
   attempt state `sending` before the network call; classify outcomes as pre-transfer
   failure, temporary rejection, permanent rejection, acknowledged acceptance (provider id
   and time) or uncertain (connection lost after DATA, or a lease expired while
   `sending`); temporary failures retry through the job store; uncertain is never retried
   automatically; a missing sender or recipient blocks with a visible fault, never
   "sent".
5. **Delivery service and decision route** (`services/deliveries.ts`, `routes/
   submission.ts`): owner-only delivery history and `POST /api/deliveries/:id/decision`
   for an uncertain attempt (mark delivered, or resend as a new attempt on the same
   revision); integrate with the T06 resend (one open attempt per revision).
