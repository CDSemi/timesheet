## Required changes (binding)

1. **Template engine** (`src/domain/emailTemplate.ts`): variables exactly
   `{EmployeeName}`, `{PeriodStart}`, `{PeriodEnd}`, `{PayrollDate}`, `{SignOffStatus}`,
   `{SubmissionId}` and `{Revision}`. An unknown variable gives 422. Values are
   HTML-escaped in the HTML body; subject lines cannot carry CR/LF.
2. **Recipients**: `to` is required, `cc` optional; validated addresses, de-duplicated
   (case-insensitive), header-injection safe; a bounded count.
3. **Submission settings service and routes** (`submissionSettings.ts`, `settings.ts`):
   - versions are append-only, with an audit record that holds no recipient list or body
     text beyond what the canonical audit rules allow;
   - an auto-submit change records its effective instant; by default a change applies to
     future periods (docs/05:26);
   - the auto-image authorization is an explicit audited act that references one
     immutable signature attachment of the same user; default off; revoking it is also
     audited;
   - a preview route renders subject and body for the user's own settings and writes
     nothing (`total_changes` and audit count unchanged);
   - owner-only access: another user's settings give 404.
   - F-4 (owner decision pending): store the per-user auto-submit effective instant as the
     T01 schema defines; do not implement the system activation here.
