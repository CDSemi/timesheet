#### Behaviour

- **Kinds.** `before_due` (24 h and 2 h by default; the user's saved `reminder_offsets_minutes` replace them, an
  empty list sends none), `overdue` (one per period, from the T10 overdue audit record, so only when auto-submit
  was off), `outcome_notice` (one per automatic revision whose timesheet has no signed revision yet). All go to
  the account address of the owner only: SMTP envelope and header carry that one address, no Cc/Bcc, and the
  payroll recipients appear nowhere in the message.
- **Outcome text** (owner decision: the employee's own notice keeps the pending disclosure): "was submitted
  automatically at the deadline (...)" and "Review is pending: it has not been signed by you yet." plus the
  login-required link. The outgoing payroll artefacts are untouched by this task.
- **Dedupe.** `reminder_occurrences` is unique per user, period, kind and occurrence key
  (`offset-<minutes>`, `overdue`, `revision-<id>`). The decision and its job are written together, so a failure
  rolls back both (tested with a trigger that fails the insert). A rerun, a second runner or a later bucket
  queues nothing new; the job business key repeats the occurrence key.
- **Stop after finalization.** The scan skips finalized and imported periods; the send handler re-checks at send
  time, so a notice queued before a manual sign-off or the automatic finalization is not sent (the job succeeds
  with nothing sent). An overdue record whose period is finalized before its scan is stored as `suppressed`.
  The outcome notice is not sent once any signed revision exists (late review).
- **Collapse.** The closest offset already reached is the one current notice (the text states the real time
  left); every other reached, undecided offset is stored `collapsed` with no job. A closer decided offset
  supersedes an offset added late. Nothing is decided at or after the deadline.
- **Activation / imported.** Nothing is read or written while `automation_active_from` is null; nothing is
  decided while the clock is before it. A deadline before it implies now < activation (notices precede the
  deadline), so one lower bound covers both. `imported_unverified` periods, deactivated accounts and finalized
  periods get nothing.
- **DST.** Deadlines come from `payPeriodAt`/`payPeriodForPayrollDate` in the saved reporting zone (including
  payroll exceptions); offsets are elapsed seconds (R-07). The tests put a deadline on the Sunday of each change
  (autumn `2026-11-01 12:00`, spring `2027-03-14 12:00`, plus a deadline inside the spring gap) via payroll
  exceptions: the reminder instants are exactly 24 h and 2 h before the production deadline (equal to the
  independent `instantOfWallTime` oracle), their wall clock is one hour off the deadline's across the change,
  and the notice text states the deadline with zone and UTC offset. No offset or season is hard-coded.
- **Links.** `reviewLink(base, payrollDate)` = `<publicBaseUrl>/#/review/<payrollDate>` only. The base must be
  plain http(s) with no credentials, query or fragment; `renderReminder` refuses any other link. Tests extract
  every URL from every captured message: exactly the one deep link, no `?`, no user id, no address, no
  token/magic wording. Job payloads hold routing identifiers only (`pay_period_id`, `kind`, `occurrence_key`).
- **Delivery.** Same adapters as submissions (capture by default); the capture folder is keyed by the job id.
  Accepted ends the job; a temporary failure retries through the job store; a permanent failure, an uncertain
  outcome and an unexpected throw go to visible intervention and are never resent; `capture_exists` (the same
  job re-run after a crash) counts as sent; a missing sender gives `sender_missing`. A job whose decision, owner
  or period does not match (forged payload, another user's occurrence) fails permanently and sends nothing.

#### Interpretations and decisions to confirm

1. "24 h before" is elapsed time, not "the same wall-clock time the day before" (R-07: durations are UTC seconds).
2. The scan job is per five-minute bucket (not per minute like T10): reminders are advisory, and it keeps the
   retained `jobs` rows low (jobs are never deleted; the retention decision of T10 now covers both scans).
3. `OutboundMessage` requires a `pdf` field; a reminder passes an empty array and the SHA-256 of empty input, so
   the capture folder of a reminder contains a zero-byte `attachment.pdf`. Making `pdf` optional belongs to
   `message.ts`/`captureAdapter.ts` (not owned here); no behaviour depends on it.
4. With real SMTP, a crash in the milliseconds between provider acceptance and `completeJob` could repeat one
   advisory reminder (capture mode is protected by its folder). No table or migration was allowed here, and the
   delivery attempts of submissions are deliberately not reused, so reminders do not appear in the employee's
   delivery history. Revisit if the owner wants at-most-once reminders on SMTP.
5. docs/05 does not mention the five-minute bucket or the reminder wording; no document was edited (not owned).
