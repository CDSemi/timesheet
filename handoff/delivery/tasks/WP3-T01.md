#### Schema points that depend on owner decisions

- F-4: one system `operations_state.automation_active_from` (NULL) plus a per-user
  `submission_settings.auto_submit_effective_from` (NOT NULL per version). If the owner wants
  per-user activation only, a migration 0005 adds it and the system column can stay unused.
- F-2: pending debits stay immutable `revision_ledger_lines` rows. There is no resolution
  column, and only a later revision re-evaluates them. A background resolution would need a new
  linked table in 0005, because the lines cannot be updated.
- F-1 and F-3: no schema dependency. A deadline revision may have zero lines. Admin totals are
  queries over `jobs` and `delivery_attempts`.
