**Schema (migration 0013 `ot_opening_balance`).**
- It rebuilds `ot_ledger` with the documented 12-step procedure:

  - The old table is dropped and the new one renamed.
  - The 3 indexes and the 2 append-only triggers are recreated byte for byte.
  - The correction-target trigger also accepts `opening_balance` as an original.
  - A new partial unique index `ot_ledger_one_opening_balance(user_id)` holds one
    opening balance per user.
- CHECKs:
  - An opening balance has a manual origin, an as-of date, an evidence reference and a
    reason, and no work date.
  - Only an opening balance has `as_of_date`.
  - Only an opening balance or a correction has `evidence_ref`.
- The self-reference names `ot_ledger`, so the foreign keys are unchanged after the
  rename. `revision_ledger_lines` is untouched.
- **Deviation: `defer_foreign_keys` alone cannot work.** Evidence 03 is a probe:
  `foreign_key_check` was empty, yet COMMIT failed. DROP TABLE's implicit DELETE counts
  deferred violations, and the rename never clears them. So `migrate()` now follows the
  documented step 1:
  - it turns `foreign_keys` OFF before BEGIN EXCLUSIVE and restores it in `finally`;
  - when anything was applied, it runs `foreign_key_check` before COMMIT, and any
    violation throws a MigrationError and rolls back.
