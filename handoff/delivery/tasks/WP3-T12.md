#### What was built

- Screen `#/review/{payrollDate}`: 14 days as one semantic table (labelled blocks below 768px, no sideways
  scroll) with category, leave, notes, session times in the saved reporting zone, and regular, off-calendar and
  credit minutes exactly as the server sent them (the client adds or rounds nothing); OT proposals; deficit days
  with a required deduct or waive radio group where the mode is choose_at_signoff, the engine decision otherwise;
  missing evidence with the acknowledgement; due and not-yet-due OT-leave reservations (link to the OT screen;
  signing never spends them); recipients, subject and the email body as plain text from the payload (never parsed
  as markup); the signature preview (`img` from `/api/signatures/{id}`, owner-scoped on the server); the sign-off
  form with the name and "Sign off & Submit", posting `expected_version` and `reviewed_hash`. Modes follow the
  finalization state: first sign-off, late review of an automatic submission (email choice) and a correction
  (reason and email choice, no default). Labels above inputs, errors below with `aria-invalid` and
  `aria-describedby`, focus on the first missing field, one request at a time (ref guard plus busy button).
- Stale handling: 409 `stale_version`, `stale_review`, `already_finalized` (also `not_finalized`,
  `no_pending_review`) reload a fresh review and show a focused alert ("Nothing was submitted ..."); the
  acknowledgement and deficit choices are cleared (they belonged to the old content), typed name and reason stay;
  `content_changed` is refused with a pointer to a correction; a 422 maps to its field (name, signature,
  acknowledgement, deficit choices, reason). An identical retry (200 `replayed`) shows "Already submitted".
- Deep link: `useHashRoute` returns `{id, payrollDate}`; `#/review/{date}` parses strictly (calendar date only, no
  query or extra segment), survives the sign-in form (the address is untouched), sits under Timesheet in the
  navigation and holds no token. A date that is not the account's own shows "No such period for your account"
  (server 404); `#/review/not-a-date` falls back to the timesheet.
- Status: `PeriodHeader` shows review status (Draft, Submitted manually, Submitted automatically with review
  pending, reviewed late, Corrected (revision n)) and delivery status (not requested, queued, needs attention,
  sending, accepted, retrying, failed, uncertain with decision needed) from `GET .../finalization` and
  `GET /api/deliveries?revision_id=` only, plus a link to the review whose wording follows the status. The same
  badges sit on the review screen. `TimesheetGrid.tsx` was not changed: `TimesheetScreen.tsx` (not owned) passes it
  only `days`, so the status lives in the header directly above the grid.
