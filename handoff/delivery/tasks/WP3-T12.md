## Required changes (binding)

1. **Review screen** (`ReviewScreen.tsx`, `components/Review*.tsx`, pure
   `components/reviewModel.ts`): the exact review content from the GET payload — the 14
   days with categories, times and minutes as the server returns them (the client
   computes no business minutes), OT proposals, missing evidence and unresolved inputs,
   per-day deficit choices where the policy mode is choose, unconsumed OT-leave
   reservations (E-3), recipients and the rendered email preview, the signature preview,
   and the incomplete-evidence acknowledgement; "Sign off & Submit" requires the signer
   name; it posts `expected_version` and `reviewed_hash`.
2. **Stale review:** a 409 (`stale_version`, `stale_review`, `already_finalized`) leads to
   a fresh review with a clear message; nothing is submitted twice; a 422 shows the
   missing field.
3. **Deep link:** `#/review/{payrollDate}` survives login (redirect back after sign-in);
   no token in the URL.
4. **Status in the grid and header:** review and delivery status per period (draft,
   submitted manually, submitted automatically with review pending, delivery state) in
   `PeriodHeader.tsx`/`TimesheetGrid.tsx`, from server fields only.
5. **UI standard:** every new length, colour, weight, letter-spacing, duration or shadow
   part is a custom property in `src/client/styles.css` (only `@media` conditions stay
   literal); the 4px radius token; the shared `--transition` on hover/active/focus;
   accessible labels and focus order; mobile-first layout that works at 390×844 and
   1280×800.
