## Risks and optional improvements (not proven defects)

- **R-UX5B-1, cross-revision in-flight sends (pre-existing).** The 409 "in progress" check is per revision (`deliveries.ts:420-437`, as docs/03 says). Probe `52-cross-revision.txt`: revision 1's send was still waiting for its PDF; a correction (revision 2) was signed and sent without a reason (no copy counted yet); the runner then delivered revision 2 first and the superseded revision 1 two minutes later. Payroll can thus receive the old copy after the corrected one, and the second copy needed no reason. The base behaved the same (a correction with `send_email` never checked older sends). docs/05:127 "A send still in progress blocks another one (409)" reads as period-wide. Options: block a send while any revision of the period has a waiting send, or skip a superseded revision's queued send, or clarify docs/05. Owner or coordinator call.
- **R-UX5B-2, dev-only advisories.** Full `npm audit`: 6 (4 moderate, 2 high), all dev-only: vite 8.3.1 and its source-map-js (build tool and dev server), vitest 5.0.2 / `@vitest/mocker`, and smtp-server 3.19.17 with its nested nodemailer 10.0.14. None is in the image (production dependencies only). The nested nodemailer parses addresses only inside the local loopback test sink, and the app's own address rule never hands it a comment, quote, ";" or ">"; the base had the same class (7 including the production nodemailer). Acceptable dev-only risk; a later dependency round can bump vite 8.3.5, vitest 5.0.5 and smtp-server 3.21.1.
- **R-UX5B-3, drill fetch sites.** FIX6 added `connection: close` to the drill's `call()` only (`container-drill.mjs:170-177`); `uploadSignature` (:275), `uploadWorkbook` (:302), the stage-1 PDF download (:385) and the previous-build health poll (:987) still use pooled keep-alive sockets that a later blocking `docker` spawn could strand (DIAG3's smoke failure mode). The drill passed twice since (FIX6 stages 1-3 and 6; REGATE4 `--wp3` 209/0). Harness only; harden when the drill is next touched.
- **R-UX5B-4, the worker cap and the lazy PDF import.** `maxWorkers: '50%'` rests on one discriminating DIAG3 set (4 CPUs: 31 forks 6 failures, 16 or 8 forks 0); FIX6's own before-run did not reproduce, REGATE4 passed at 4 CPUs, and my two runs (16 forks, with the other audit running) passed in 53 and 57 s.
