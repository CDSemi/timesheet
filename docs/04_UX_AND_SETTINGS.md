# UX and settings

## Screens

| Screen | Behavior |

Use hours/minutes, never 1.30 for 1h30. Display current viewing zone and saved accounting date. Keep ordinary flows free of DB/job terminology. Actual clock values are never stored from a schedule: a time becomes an actual value only when the person saves it. The new-session dialog pre-fills the start and end with the reference hours of the policy effective on that date (wall-clock times on the accounting date in the saved reporting zone, shown in the input zone) and, for a date before today in the reporting zone (the server's date), the suggested breaks that lie inside them; pre-filled values are marked as such and stay editable, and Save asks for confirmation first.

## Proposed defaults

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Normal calendar / reporting zone | Mon–Fri / America/Los_Angeles |
| Display zone | Device/current zone; does not affect calculation |
| Reference / required work | 08:00–17:00 / 480 minutes |
| Breaks | 15+30+15 excluded; shift-relative suggestions requiring confirmation (pre-filled in the new-session dialog for a day before today, confirmed by Save) |
| N / M | 30 strictly exceeded / 30 nearest, exact midpoint down |
| Non-working-calendar time | All eligible, no B/N, same M |
| Deficit | ignore; optional auto_deduct or choose_at_signoff |
| Payroll / due | Anchor 2026-10-02, every 14 days; prior Tuesday 17:00 |
| Reminder offsets | 24 hours and 2 hours |
| Unsigned auto-submit | Enabled preference after sender setup; real sending remains disabled until activation |
| Automatic signature image | Asked at signature upload as one explicit, audited authorization, pre-selected; if unticked, no image on automatic submissions until the user authorizes it later in Settings |
| Automatic submission note line | Off; when on, the PDF and email show the user's text (default "Automatic submission") |
| Show OT on PDF | On; can hide without erasing ledger |
| Notifications / outbound | Email, optional ntfy / dry-run initially |

These are declared design defaults, not claims that every value was user-confirmed. Calculation/calendar settings need an effective date. Preview changes to future/draft defaults, preserve explicit overrides and immutable revisions, and warn if next year's company calendar is missing.

## Timesheet sheet

The Timesheet page shows the form of the Excel workbook and the PDF. The header block carries the title, "Employee:", "Payroll Date:" and "Period:"; the company name is in the top bar and is not repeated on the on-screen sheet (the PDF keeps its company header). Two bands, "WEEK 1" and "WEEK 2", each a square sheet with a header strip (week number and range) and running Monday to Sunday with the rows Day, Date, Category, Time, OT (h:mm) and Status. The header strip and the first column (the row names Day, Date, Category, Time, OT, Status and the worked rows) have a slightly stronger fill (`--sheet-head-strong`) than the date cells: 1.27:1 from the date cell in the light theme and 1.36:1 in both dark sets, with `--text` at 12.30:1 (light) and 9.91:1 (dark) and `--muted` at 4.59:1 (light) and 4.83:1 (dark) on it. Status is the only row the Excel form lacks: it only displays each day's status in words and a shape, never by colour alone (Complete, Running, Open session, Confirm breaks, No times, Missing record, Upcoming, Calculation problem); it is not a checkbox, and "No times" is drawn as a ring with a diagonal bar, not as an empty box. The "Show worked hours" switch (off when the page opens, not stored; named in round 4 for what it adds, exactly two rows) adds the rows "Worked on a workday" and "Worked on a day off". Below the weeks sit a legend, the "Overtime Total :" box and the signature lines.

- Title gap (round 4, owner answer F-5): from 768px the title row of Timesheet and of Review is `--page-head-gap` (32px, `--space-6`) above the next block (the period card, and on Review the first block under the title); below 768px the head is as before.
- Paper sheet (round 4, owner answer F-5): the toolbar, the page messages, the batch bar, the form header, both week sheets, the legend, Overtime Total and the signature lines stand on one surface, the paper (`[data-paper]`; on Review it holds the "What you sign" heading, its hint and the sheet, and the checklist stays outside). The title row and the period card stay outside it. The paper is `--paper-surface` (the card colour), raised by `--elev-paper` (the `--elev-2` step), with the card corner (`--radius-lg`, 12px) and `--paper-pad` (16px) of padding from 768px; it differs from the page by at least 1.12:1 in both themes and clips nothing, so a lifted column's shadow, an open category list and the editor column lift draw over it. The toolbar, the week sheets and the footer keep their own style, layout and colours: the week sheets and the footer lose only their outer shadow (their 1px frame and header strip stay), and the signature and date lines stay in their two rows. On a phone (below 768px) the paper is full-bleed and square: it reaches both window edges (negative inline margin equal to the page padding) with the same 16px of side padding, so the four-column table keeps the width it had without the paper (358px at 390px), nothing scrolls sideways, and the PDF does not change. On Review the checklist sits beside the paper from 1330px (the page widens to `--page-max-editor`, 1600px, as it does for the open Day editor, so the sheet keeps its 850px; it was 1200px on a page capped at 1200px, where the sheet had only 772px) and follows it below 1330px.

- Category cell: the app category (Worked, Off, Vacation, Sick, Holiday, Shutdown), the holiday name for a Holiday, a second line for work from home or for OT-funded leave minutes, and a note marker. Excel's "Sick Day" and "Off Day (Overtime Used)" are not reintroduced: OT leave stays leave minutes with `leave_kind` ot (document 10, 2026-10-03 E-2). A day with no record shows no placeholder text; empty cells are blank.
- Every number is a server field shown as h:mm. The client computes no business minutes. The OT cell follows the PDF rule: the credited minutes of a complete day, "pending" while the day is incomplete or its breaks are unconfirmed, blank for a day without a record. "Overtime Total :" is the server's provisional credited total over all 14 days, including both Sundays, with a note when days are still pending; it equals the PDF total. The Excel formulas that conflict with the canonical rules are not reproduced (document 10, 2026-10-08).
- Formats are those of the PDF: US dates (MM/DD in the cells, MM/DD/YYYY in the header and period bar), durations as h:mm, times as 24-hour ranges such as 08:00-17:00 in the display zone, with several sessions listed and a running session marked. The accounting date stays the saved reporting-zone date.
- Non-working days (weekends, holidays and closures from the server calendar, never inferred from the weekday) carry one light tint with fine diagonal hatching. Today carries an accent top bar and a "Today" tag. A cell that needs input has an amber tint plus the Status text and shape. Columns chosen in batch mode and the column open in the Day editor are lifted: raised by a few pixels over a soft shadow with a 4px corner, never boxed in a heavy ring. A batch-selected column also has a light tint and its checked selection box; consecutive lifted days of a week form one group with one shadow around the group and none cast on a neighbour. The day open in the Day editor is marked as a whole by the same lift plus a 1px accent frame inside the column and a filled Day cell (a 1px frame on the row and a filled Day cell on a phone, where selection is a tint and the checked box without lift), and its day button carries `aria-current`. The marker combines with the Today bar and the batch selection on the same column, goes when the editor closes or when the day is not on the displayed sheet, keeps at least 3:1 contrast for the frame (measured 4.82:1 or more on every column surface beside the sheet; the drawer has no backdrop in either layout, so nothing dims the frame and the 3:1 figure no longer depends on the layout) and 4.5:1 for text, and never covers the open category picker. Under reduced motion the lift is static. Every day column is at least 108px wide (`--sheet-col-min`) so no word is cut inside the cell; from 768 to 929px, where seven columns do not fit the page and the paper's padding, the week card scrolls sideways inside itself and the page never does (it was 899px before the paper added 32px).
- Signature lines: "Employee Signature" shows the signer's name when signed, or the pending-review state, with the date in the saved reporting zone and a link to the Review. "Manager Signature" shows "Not used yet" with an empty date. The signature image never appears on the Timesheet page. The lines and the link are absent in a shared view and for an imported period.
- Phones (below 768px): each week is a four-column table Day | Category | Time | OT, one row per day at least 54px tall, with no sideways scroll; the Status folds into the Day cell and the Time cell tint. A whole row opens the day editor, and the row of the day open in it is framed. The first screen shows the period card (with "<" and ">" beside the title, and "Today" and the Review link together in one row) and the compact tools, so the first day row is fully visible above the tab bar at 390x844 (measured in round 4, with the paper sheet's 12px of top padding: its bottom is at 571px, or 670px with the zone note, against the tab bar at 788px; 623px and 722px with a received share; it was 559px, 658px, 611px and 710px without the paper), also in the five days when "Current period" joins that row (down to the narrowest phones, "Current" and "Sign off" keep the one row; see "Phone buttons"); the check in/out controls are in the top bar, not on the page; the page heading and the form title stay for screen readers only. Both layouts expose exactly one element per day, named by weekday and date, with one day button whose accessible name holds its visible text and keeps the accounting date (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.3): "Edit {MM/DD} ({date})" on the sheet, for example "Edit 09/28 (2026-09-28)", and "Edit {weekday} {MM/DD} ({date})" on a phone, for example "Edit Mon 09/28 (2026-09-28)"; "View" replaces "Edit" when read-only.
- The Review uses the same sheet. In a shared view, the share bar stays above the period bar and controls outside the share are absent.

## Period bar, check in/out and batch mode

- Period bar: a two-row card from 768px. Row one has the title (the period dates joined by an arrow, "09/28/2026 → 10/11/2026", which screen readers read as "to") with a coloured pill (old amber, current green, future pink, or "in progress" blue for the period that contains today while the period before it is still the current one; a status such as "Draft" is a neutral grey pill) and the buttons Previous, Next, Today, Current period (when it applies) and "Review & sign off", in that order (the Review link is disabled with its reason for an imported period). Row two, under a hairline, holds the payroll date, the due date in words with the reporting zone, where "Due" is a label in the same style as "Payroll date" and the date and time keep the value style (for example "Due Tue 12/08/2026, 17:00 (America/Los_Angeles)"), and the viewing-zone phrase; these parts share one line when there is room and wrap onto further lines when there is not. The card carries exactly one status group (review and delivery). The bar always names the current viewing zone compactly (for example "Times in America/Los_Angeles"), also when it equals the reporting zone; each day in the sheet keeps its saved accounting date. The reporting zone, display zone, the due time in the display zone and the explanation of accounting dates versus session times show in an extra note only when the display zone differs from the reporting zone. A banner says when edits to the period require a reason.
- Today: always shown. It opens the period that contains today's date in the timesheet's saved reporting zone (the server's date, never the device zone), shows today's column and moves an open Day editor to today. It is inactive (`aria-disabled`, still focusable, dimmed with colours) when the sheet already shows today and the editor, if open, is on today; a press on the inactive button re-reads the server's date and moves to today's period only when the date has changed (for example after midnight in the reporting zone), otherwise nothing moves. The periods are also re-read when the window regains focus or the tab becomes visible.
- Current period: returns to the period the page opens on, the current period of R-07 (document 02). It is shown only in the five days from the Monday after a period ends to its payroll Friday, and only while the sheet shows another period; it only navigates and leaves the editor alone. In a shared view both buttons use the owner's zone and dates. Each jump is announced in a hidden polite status region.
- Phone buttons: Previous and Next stay beside the title; Today, Current period (when shown) and the Review link share one row at every phone width down to 320px. Each button stays 44px high on one line and shows its full label when that fits and a short form otherwise: "Sign off" for "Review & sign off" (and "Review" for "Review now" and "Review or correct") below about 22.8rem of row width, then "Current" for "Current period" below about 18.4rem; only below about 15.4rem (no real phone) does the Review link take its own row with its full label. The accessible names are always the full labels ("Current period", "Review & sign off"). Desktop shows the full labels.
- Check in/out: one state-aware button. Checked out shows "Checked out" and "Check in"; checked in shows "Checked in since HH:MM" with a static ringed dot and "Check out", which opens the break confirmation. On a desktop it is a group at the right end of the Timesheet page's title row (the state words right-aligned over the date line, no card). On a phone the same state and button are a group in the top bar on every screen of the person's own context (the bar is 60px high, or 112px when the "Shared with me" switcher takes a second row), so the group is not on the page; the break confirmation is the same dialog and focus returns to the button that opened it. It is absent in every shared view, on any width (the owner's check in/out stays the owner's).
- Toolbar: "Open a day" (its words, the date field and the "Open day" button on one row at every width; on the narrowest row, below 21rem, the words are hidden and the field keeps its accessible name; any date, also outside the displayed period; Today brings the sheet back), the "Show worked hours" switch and "Change several days" (an outlined button until pressed, set apart from the switch by a 24px gap and a hairline separator from 768px). Batch mode is off by default: the selection boxes, "Select all", "Clear", the category choice ("Category for selected days") and "Preview changes" appear only after "Change several days" and leave with "Done". Batch category changes still preview conflicts with recorded work before saving. Without edit rights (view-only share) and for an imported period these controls are absent or disabled with their reason.

## Day editor

- Drawer (round 4, owner request of 2026-10-20, U4-05 to U4-07): from 768px the editor is a non-modal drawer (no backdrop, nothing inert, no `aria-modal`; the page stays usable), flush with one window edge from under the top bar to the bottom of the window. Docked right its radius is `--radius-lg 0 0 --radius-lg`: the two corners on the window edge are square and the two toward the sheet are 12px; docked left is the mirror. Phones (below 768px): a modal bottom sheet of at most 86% of the screen height with square bottom corners.
- Beside or floating: the Day editor floats only when the sheet and the drawer would not fit side by side, a rule of the window width alone (never of the mode the editor is in, so it cannot flap). `editorLayout` (`editorLayoutModel.ts`) returns `bottom-sheet` below 768px, `beside` when `viewportWidth - pagePadding - editorWidth - gap >= sheetMinWidth`, otherwise `floating`. `viewportWidth` is `document.documentElement.clientWidth` (a classic scrollbar counts); every other input is read in px from a token: `--page-pad` (16px each side), `--editor-width` (400px), `--editor-gap` (16px) and `--sheet-min-width` (the 92px label column, 7 x `--sheet-col-min` 108px, the 2px of hairlines and 2 x `--paper-pad` 16px: 882px). With the documented tokens the switch is at 1330px with overlay scrollbars (measured by the sweep test) and 1345px of window width with a classic 15px scrollbar; it replaces the fixed 1360px of round 3, which was 30 to 47px too early. Beside: the drawer is fixed on the right and the page reserves `--editor-width + --editor-gap` on that side, so the sheet is never under the drawer (16px or more clear) and every day column is at least 108px wide. Floating: the drawer lies over the sheet's edge, docked at the left edge instead of the right when the edited column lies in the right half of the screen, with the edited column scrolled into view. The page is not inert, so a control behind the drawer can take focus: when its box lies wholly under the drawer, the drawer docks to the other edge (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11), so a focused control is never entirely hidden.
- Switching and unselecting: a press on another column's day button, or anywhere on that column, switches the drawer to that day without closing it first; a press (or Enter) on the open day's own button closes it, focus stays on that button and a polite status says "Day editor closed". When the Day fields form or the reason holds edits that are not saved, every action that would change the open day, close the drawer or move the sheet to another period first asks "Discard your changes?" (Keep editing and Discard) through the confirmation dialog: a switch, an unselect, "Open a day", "Today", "Previous period", "Next period", "Current period", the Close button and Escape (a nested dialog keeps Escape first). Keep editing leaves the drawer, its edits and the period shown exactly as they were; Discard goes ahead (a period step leaves the drawer open and starts it again from the saved day). With nothing unsaved, nothing asks. Saved fields and a reason already used by a save are not unsaved edits. The session dialog is modal, so no switch can happen while it is open. Focus moves to the drawer heading on open; the drawer follows the sheet in the DOM (Tab from the sheet reaches it, Shift+Tab leaves it); Escape closes the drawer wherever focus is (a nested dialog takes Escape first), as does Close, and focus returns to the day's date button. While the drawer is open, the day it edits is marked on the sheet (see "Timesheet sheet").
- Header: on its own surface, with the title "Mon 10/12/2026" (the dialog name keeps the ISO accounting date), a Close icon button and chips: the period (current, old, future or in progress), "Times in {display zone}", "Day in {reporting zone}" only when the display zone differs from the reporting zone, and "View only" for a read-only share. There is no long note about zones; the explanation of the input zone sits beside the "Input zone" field of the session dialog.
- Order: the header, a status banner, the stale/notice/error messages and the reason field (old or finalized periods), "Times" (one card per session with its range, a state pill, its source line and a break table, plus Add session), "Category and leave" and "Figures (computed by the server)". The panel only shows the chosen times and breaks; the explicit end date and the visible "Input zone" are in the session dialog, and the day fields keep their own button ("Save day fields").
- Session cards: the range in the display zone (`+1` when the end is on a later local day), a pill ("Breaks confirmed" or "No breaks taken" green, "Breaks not confirmed" amber, "Running" with a static dot), a source line ("Entered by hand in {zone}", "Checked in" while the session runs, or "Checked in and out") and the break table (Break, Date, Start, Minutes, in the display zone). A session with unconfirmed breaks shows "OT stays pending until breaks are confirmed." and a "Confirm breaks" button that opens the session dialog with its saved rows or, when it has none, the suggested breaks that lie inside the session; if none fits, Save asks through the notice below. The old one-tap buttons no longer exist. Edit and Delete are absent in a read-only share or an imported period.
- Session dialog: Add session, Edit and Confirm breaks open a modal dialog (720px wide, full screen below 768px) titled "New session" or "Edit session" with the accounting date and reporting zone, a Time section (the "Input zone" with its short explanation is the first field, then the start and end date and time, then the overnight note; the order is the DOM, the visual and the Tab order) and a Breaks section; in an old or finalized period it shows the reason field. "Today" and "Now" (round 4): a quiet link-style button sits under each date ("Today", accessible name "Today, start date") and each time ("Now"), right-aligned to the input above it (its right edge is the input's). "Today" sets that date to the current date in the dialog's input zone; "Now" sets the time to the current minute in the input zone with `:00` seconds and, when the field holds another date, the date too, so the value is this instant. The instant comes from the device clock and is converted with the input zone, never the device zone (rule 7); the server's five-minute future allowance still applies. A change through a link counts as a typed change: the draft is dirty, a fold or offset is dropped and, in a new session, the suggestions follow. The links are 44px targets on touch, are disabled while the zone is not a valid IANA name and do not exist where there is no dialog (a view-only share). Running session (round 4): Edit on today's running session opens this dialog with "running" in its subtitle and, in place of End, the box "No end yet: this session is running. It ends when you check out." with a quiet "Set an end instead" (it switches to the ended-session flow, the way to close a forgotten check out; absent in a shared view, because a grantee never checks the owner in or out). The breaks show their date field (there is no end yet), are checked against the start only and have no "Confirm no breaks taken" box; the hint reads "You confirm the breaks when you check out". Save sends `end: null`, `breaks_confirmed: false` and the loaded version, asks no "No breaks listed" question and ends in "Save this session?" with the time as "07:45 - running" and the breaks as "1 break, confirmed at Check out"; Check out afterwards counts the worked minutes from the new start. A start later than now plus five minutes and an overlap with another session are refused by the server and shown in the alert below. A new session is pre-filled (see the rule at the top): the reference hours and, only for a date before today in the reporting zone as the server reports it (never the device clock), the suggested breaks inside the session, with a note, a "suggested" tag on untouched suggestions, and suggestions that follow the start time until a break is edited. On a phone the head and the two 44px buttons stay in view while the body scrolls.
- Break rows: one row per break with "Break n", start time, minutes (1 to 720) and a remove button; the end is computed ("ends 10:15") because a break's end is its start plus its minutes (elapsed time, R-07). The break date field appears only when the session crosses local midnight or has no end yet; otherwise a break is on the session's date. One checkbox, "Confirm no breaks taken", hides and disables the rows (kept and restored when unticked); "Suggest breaks" and "Add break" add rows. A nonexistent or ambiguous local time raises the usual prompt naming "Break n". A row is one line from 42rem in fixed column slots (round 4): "Break n" (`--break-num-w`), the date when shown (`--break-date-w`), the time (`flex: 0 0 var(--break-time-slot-w)`, 9.6rem: "12:00:00 AM" with its picker icon needs 131px in en-US with a pointer and 139px on touch, measured with a probe field; `--break-time-w` stays the 6.8rem least width of the two-line row that the chip threshold counts), the minutes (`--break-min-w`), then the info slot ("ends 10:15", "suggested"; it always renders and reads "ends --:--" until the row resolves) taking the rest, then the remove button, so the time and minutes inputs have the same x and width in every row whether it is saved, suggested or new; no time text is clipped. It is two lines below that ("Break n" with its info, then the fields), and below the measured width (a list of 268px for a same-day row, 390px for an overnight row) each break is a chip such as "Break 1 · 10:00 → 10:15" (24-hour; the start date only for an overnight session) that opens a "Break n" sub-dialog with date (if shown), start time, minutes, Remove break, Cancel and Done; nothing is saved until the session is saved. Row and server errors mark the row or chip.
- Save and cancel: Save validates, then, with no break listed and the box unticked, a notice "No breaks listed" offers "Confirm no breaks taken", "Leave breaks unconfirmed for now" and "Go back and add breaks" (Escape is Go back); a save always ends with a confirmation "Save this session?" listing day, time and breaks, with Back and Save. Saving confirms the listed breaks (or none); an unconfirmed save keeps breaks unknown and OT pending. Cancel, Close and Escape ask "Discard your changes?" (Keep editing, Discard) only when something changed. A failed save (a server refusal such as an overlap, a start or end in the future or an accounting-date mismatch, no answer from the server, a form message such as an empty end, or break rows that cannot be saved) opens the alert dialog "This session was not saved" (round 4, `AlertDialog`) with the message, a hint and one OK button; row errors are listed by "Break n". OK has the initial focus and Escape acts as OK; on closing, focus goes to the first marked row's field (or chip), else to Save. The row and field markers and the daylight-saving questions stay inline; no error paragraph is left at the end of the dialog body. A stale version keeps its own reload flow. At most one modal is on top at a time and Escape closes only that one; focus returns to the control that opened it, or to the "Times" heading after a save.
- Delete: "Delete" opens an alert dialog "Delete this session?" listing day, time and breaks, with "Cancel" (initial focus) and "Delete session"; in an old or finalized period it shows the reason field and waits for it.
- Check out dialog: the same break rows and chips under the title "Check out", with the "Confirm no breaks taken" box. The date field is always shown there (a running session has no end yet). The buttons say what happens: "Check out, no breaks taken", "Check out with these breaks" (the listed rows replace the saved ones) and "Check out, breaks unconfirmed" (an empty list and an unticked box: the breaks stay unknown and OT stays pending. When the dialog opened with no saved row the list is omitted. When it opened with saved rows and the person removed every one, Check out sends the empty unconfirmed list, which replaces and so deletes them, and the dialog says so: "The saved breaks you removed are deleted at Check out. When its rows or the "Confirm no breaks taken" box changed, Cancel or Escape asks "Discard your changes?" A failed Check out (a refusal, no answer, or rows that cannot be saved) opens the same alert "This session was not saved" with one OK button, as the session dialog does; focus then goes to the first marked row, else to the Check out button, and a stale version keeps its Reload panel. The input zone is not a field of this dialog.
- WFH: in "Category and leave" the "Worked from home (WFH)" checkbox is centred on the Category field (its label is as tall as the field, 1px at most off its centre, also on a phone where both are 44px) and wraps below it only on the narrowest widths. ## Editing and review

Save in the session dialog confirms the listed breaks or the ticked "Confirm no breaks taken"; Check out does the same with its listed rows or box, and an empty list with the box unticked keeps breaks unknown. Unknown breaks or open intervals keep OT pending. Show explicit end date for overnight entry. Batch category changes must expose existing work conflicts and never silently delete clock evidence. Holiday work retains holiday classification and actual intervals.

Current/future draft edits audit automatically without a reason; old/finalized edits require one. Review flags attendance assumptions, incomplete OT and proposed deficits. The employee may acknowledge incomplete optional clock evidence and submit attendance. Invalid records require correction or explicit audited exclusion. Automatic submission uses valid saved attendance; for a period with no saved entries it uses the default labels (FR-03). Days without records credit no OT and create no deficit; incomplete days keep OT pending. The employee may add records later and correct the period (document 05).

Leave: the Day editor shows a non-blocking warning when the day's `ot`-kind leave minutes differ from the linked leave request's consumed minutes. It never spends or releases OT. The only way to consume reserved OT leave is the employee's "Record use" action; reservations not yet used stay visible as reserved.

## Visual standard

Plain CSS custom properties in `src/client/styles.css`; system font stack only (the content security policy allows no web fonts), with numbers, times and OT in the monospace stack. New values are added as custom properties, never as ad hoc values.

- Style (round 3, owner decision of 2026-10-15; it replaces the industrial direction, the 4px radius and the 300 ms transition of E-8): a modern, friendly business style. Surfaces step from the page (`--bg`) to cards (`--card`), then sheets, panels and menus. Every colour has a light value in `:root` and the same dark value in both dark sets; a card differs from the page by at least 1.12:1 in both themes. One brand accent (`--accent`); status colours and the period pills (`--pill-*`: old amber, current green, future pink, in progress blue, neutral grey) always carry words or a shape.
- Shape (radius scale): inputs and chips `--radius-sm` 6px; buttons and inset panels `--radius` 8px; cards, the period bar, dialogs, the editor drawer (its two corners toward the sheet; the two on the window edge are square) and menus `--radius-lg` 12px; pills and the avatar `--radius-pill`. The paper sheet takes the card corner (`--radius-lg`) from 768px and is square on a phone, where it is full-bleed. Exception: the week sheets and the sheet footer are square (`--radius-sheet`, 0), because the sheet keeps the Excel form; a lifted (selected or open) sheet column has a 4px corner (`--radius-lift`). Switches are round: the track takes `--radius-pill` and the thumb is a circle.
- Elevation: a hairline ring plus soft layered shadows, never a hard border. `--elev-bar` the top bar, `--elev-1` cards and the period bar, `--elev-2` the paper sheet (`--elev-paper`; the week sheets and the footer card on it cast no shadow of their own), `--elev-3` menus, dialogs and the editor drawer (beside the sheet or floating), `--elev-lift` a lifted sheet column. The dark levels carry a light ring because a shadow alone is invisible on a dark page. The Timesheet and Review pages stand the toolbar, the form header, each week sheet (with its header strip of week number and range) and the footer on one paper sheet, so the sheet stands out from the page.
- Round 4 tokens (owner answer F-5 and the round-4 controls): `--page-head-gap` 32px (the title gap from 768px); `--paper-surface`, `--elev-paper`, `--paper-pad` 16px and `--paper-gap` 16px (the paper sheet); `--sheet-head-strong` (the week header and first-column fill, in the light set and both dark sets); `--select-arrow-size` 7px, `--select-arrow-inset` 12px and `--select-pad-end` 34px (every select); the switch tokens `--switch-width` 44px, `--switch-height` 24px, `--switch-thumb` 18px and `--switch-inset` 3px with the round track and thumb.
- Select (round 4): every select has no native arrow (`appearance: none`) and draws a chevron with two gradient layers (no `url()`), its right edge 12px from the border box, in `--muted` (5.83:1 on `--field-bg` in the light theme, 7.07:1 in both dark sets, against the 3:1 needed), with 34px of end padding so the text stays clear of it; in forced colours the native arrow returns.
- Checkbox (round 4): a checkbox is centred on the first line of its text (within 1px); on a phone or with a coarse pointer its label stays 44px tall with one line centred in it.
- Motion: one shared transition token (`--transition`) that lists only the properties that change (colour, background, border colour, box shadow, transform) and never `all`; `--duration` 180 ms (`--duration-fast` 120 ms, `--duration-slow` 260 ms) with `--ease-standard`. A control that needs another duration sets `--transition-duration` on itself. A pressed button moves by a small token offset. No entrance, scroll or looping animation; under reduced motion the durations are 0.01 ms, and the running-session dot is static.
- Buttons: primary (accent fill), secondary (card with accent border) and quiet (accent text), each with a visible focus ring (one shared token: a solid accent ring with a card-coloured gap, at least 3:1 against every surface in both themes).
- Hover and pressed: secondary and quiet buttons, navigation links, tabs, the More entries, switches (every `role="switch"`: "Show worked hours" and the two sharing switches, round track and round thumb, 44x24px, the thumb position and the track fill showing the state) and picker options share one hover tint (`--hover-fill`) with an accent boundary; a pressed (toggled-on) button is a solid accent fill with its on-accent text, and under the pointer it turns to the stronger accent, so hover never hides the pressed state. Hover looks apply only on devices that can hover (`@media (hover: hover)`), so a tap leaves none. Measurable criterion, in both themes: the hover fill differs from the resting surface by at least 1.20:1, the accent boundary reaches 3:1 against the surface, text on the fill keeps 4.5:1, and the pressed fill differs from the unpressed hover fill by at least 3:1.
- Touch: controls are at least 44px (`--tap-min`) below 768px and with a coarse pointer.
- Day-state colour roles (sheet rules and heads, non-working tint with hatching, today, needs input, selected, editing, running) have light and dark values. State is never colour alone: every state also has words or a shape.
- Brand: the logo and the wordmark are brand artwork in fixed colours, not restyled with the interface colours. The logo is a vector file; the wordmark is vector outlines drawn in the page, and no font file is shipped. In the dark theme the wordmark uses documented lighter tints of the brand blue and grey so it stays readable; the light theme keeps the brand colours.
- Mobile-first layout with readable density for a small-team timesheet app (clear hierarchy and comfortable spacing; the sheet keeps its Excel form). Light and dark themes follow the device until the person chooses one with the "Theme" control (Light or Dark, each with a sun or moon icon, in the desktop account menu and in the phone More panel); the choice is stored on that device only, with no server setting, and is applied before the page is drawn.
